🐸 Frogy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

The short version: Frogy keeps your progress on your device and backs it up to your own private cloud copy so you don't lose it. The camera blink check runs only on your device; no photos or video ever leave it. Frogy doesn't sell your data and doesn't use analytics. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.

Who makes Frogy

Frogy is made by Jonáš Fremund, the controller of your personal data. In this policy, "Frogy" means the app and its developer. Contact: appfrogy@gmail.com.

What Frogy stores and why

  • Your progress: XP, streak, path levels, lily gems, Frogy's outfits, quests, game scores and your settings. Used to run the app.
  • Eye comfort check-ins: how your eyes feel (1–5), symptoms you pick (for example dry or tired eyes, headache, blurry vision) and your own estimate of yesterday's screen time. Used only to show your charts and to choose today's exercises for you. This is health-related information, so Frogy treats it with extra care: it stays on your device, goes into your cloud backup only if you say yes, and is never used for ads or shared with anyone.
  • Activity: when you did exercises and breaks, used for your streak, charts and reminders.

All of this is saved on your device. Home-screen widgets and the lock-screen streak countdown read a small summary (streak, today's breaks) from your device too.

Cloud backup

To keep your progress safe when you change or lose your phone, Frogy backs it up using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, stored in the European Union). The app creates an anonymous account for you; it contains no name or email. If you choose Continue with Google, your backup is linked to your Google account and Frogy receives your email address and name from Google so you can sign in on other devices.

Your eye comfort check-ins are not included in the backup unless you turn on Back up my check-ins too (asked on your first check-in, and available in Settings → Progress backup). If you turn it off later, they are removed from the backup with the next save and stay only on your device.

Your backup is a single private record that only your account can read or write. You can delete it, together with the cloud account, at any time in Settings → Progress backup → Delete my cloud data. Progress on your device stays.

Camera

If you turn on the camera blink check, Frogy uses the front camera and Apple's ARKit face tracking during an exercise to detect when your eyes close and open, so it can count your blinks and check your form.

  • Face data is processed in real time, only on your device, only while an exercise is running.
  • Frogy keeps only the result (for example "18 of 20 blinks", "90 % form"). No photos, video, face data or face measurements are stored, sent to Frogy or shared with anyone.
  • Face data is never used for advertising, identification or anything other than counting blinks.

You can turn the camera off in Frogy's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Notifications and reminders

Break reminders, the morning check-in, the streak reminder and the streak countdown are scheduled on your device. No server is involved and nothing about them is sent to Frogy.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads start, Frogy asks for your consent where the law requires it (Google's consent message), and iOS asks whether Frogy may track you. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you don't, ads are not personalised. Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Frogy Plus members see no ads.

Purchases

Frogy Plus and lily gems are bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Frogy never sees your card or payment details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active.

What Frogy doesn't do

  • Frogy doesn't sell or rent your data.
  • Frogy doesn't use analytics or crash-tracking services.
  • Frogy doesn't use your check-ins or health-related answers for advertising.

Legal basis (EU/EEA)

  • Running the app and the cloud backup: performance of the agreement between you and Frogy (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Eye comfort check-ins on your device: your explicit consent, which you give by filling them in (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). Backing them up to the cloud: your separate explicit consent, given by turning on Back up my check-ins too. You can withdraw either at any time by turning the backup off, not filling in check-ins, or deleting the app or your backup.
  • Personalised ads and tracking: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can change in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. The cloud backup stays until you delete it in the app or ask Frogy to delete it. Check-ins older than about two months are removed automatically.

Your rights

You can ask Frogy for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to appfrogy@gmail.com. If you're in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority; in the Czech Republic that is the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

Children

Frogy is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given Frogy personal data, write to appfrogy@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Not medical advice

Frogy is a wellbeing app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If your eyes hurt, your vision changes or problems last, see an optometrist or doctor.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
